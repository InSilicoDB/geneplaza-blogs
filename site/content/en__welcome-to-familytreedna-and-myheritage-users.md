# **Welcome to FamilyTreeDNA and MyHeritage users**

 

We got many requests from the users from FamilyTreeDNA and MyHeritage members asked us if they could upload their DNA to our platform.

Up to now it was not possible but yes… we did the work for you… It’s possible.

## **How to upload your DNA?**

### **FamilyTreeDNA user?**

  * Upload your “** _Build 37 Raw data concatenated_** ” on our site [here](<https://www.geneplaza.com>) after you registered.
  * [Where can you find your FamilyTreeDNA data and how can you download it?](<https://www.familytreedna.com/learn/user-guide/family-finder-myftdna/download-raw-data-page/>)



### **MyHeritage user?**

  * Upload your DNA on GenePlaza website [here.](<https://www.geneplaza.com>)
  * [Where can you find your MyHeritage data and how can you download it?](<https://www.yourdnaguide.com/download-myheritage/>)
